When ISB PGP Is the Right Choice

ISB PGP is the right choice when: you specifically need campus placement infrastructure to change industry, function, or enter consulting/investment banking from a non-traditional background; you can absorb ₹55-70L total cost including foregone income; GMAT and full-time campus commitment is feasible; and ISB's Hyderabad/Mohali network is relevant to your target sector.

ISB's median placement package (~₹34L) is its strongest argument. For candidates who need a recruiter to place them into a new role in a new sector — ISB's placement infrastructure is among India's best.

When IIM Nagpur Blended MBA Is the Right Choice

IIM Nagpur is the right choice when: you cannot take 1 year off work (financial, family, career continuity); you want an IIM institutional credential (government IIM vs private deemed university ISB); you specifically need an MBA degree (ISB gives PG Diploma — not a UGC degree); or the ₹55-70L total ISB cost is prohibitive. For senior professionals (8-15 years) who are managing their own career without needing placement — IIM Nagpur delivers comparable career impact at 1/4 the total cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Nagpur or ISB better for MBA in India?

They serve different purposes. ISB PGP (₹38-40L fees, 1 year full-time) is stronger for career changers who need placement support and can absorb the full cost. IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (₹12.10L, 2 years, no income loss) is better for senior professionals managing their own career who want IIM institutional credentials without the full-time campus interruption.

Is ISB PGP a degree or diploma?

ISB PGP is a Post Graduate Programme — it awards a Post Graduate Diploma (PG Diploma), not a UGC-recognised MBA degree. ISB is a private deemed university and its PGP credential is widely recognised in industry despite not being a UGC degree. However, for roles that specifically require an 'MBA degree', the IIM Nagpur Blended MBA (UGC-DEB approved) satisfies that requirement; ISB PGP does not.

What is ISB PGP fee in 2026?

ISB PGP fee for the 2025-26 batch is approximately ₹38-40L (excluding living costs). Including accommodation and living expenses during the 1-year campus programme: approximately ₹42-45L. Plus foregone income for 1 year. Total effective cost for most working professionals: ₹55-70L.
