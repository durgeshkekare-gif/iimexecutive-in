IIM Ahmedabad AGMP vs IIM Kozhikode NAMP 2026 — Which Is Better?

August 04, 2026 · 7 min read · iimexecutive.in

AGMP vs NAMP — The Core Difference

Both are executive certificates from top IIMs for working professionals. Both give IIM alumni status. The difference is who they're designed for and what brand they carry. AGMP is from India's #1 IIM (NIRF, triple crown) targeting 5+ year professionals. NAMP is from India's #3 IIM (NIRF) targeting 10+ year professionals specifically.

IIM Ahmedabad AGMP — When This Wins

Choose AGMP when: IIMA brand is your primary objective (it is the strongest management credential in India); you have 5+ years experience; your career targets roles where IIMA alumni status is a known filter; budget allows ₹8L + GST; you want India's triple-crown accreditation on your CV.

IIM Kozhikode NAMP — When This Wins

Choose NAMP when: you have 10+ years experience and want a programme that starts from where you actually are; you want IIM-K credentials at half the AGMP fee; the curriculum's 10+ year focus makes it more relevant than a general management programme; or EQUIS+AMBA accreditation (international recognition) matters for your goals.

"At 10+ years, the question is not which IIM brand is stronger — it is which programme is designed for your experience level. NAMP's curriculum assumes 10 years of career; AGMP's is calibrated for 5-8 years."

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Ahmedabad AGMP better than IIM Kozhikode NAMP?

It depends on your experience and career goal. IIMA AGMP (₹8L, 5+ yrs exp) gives India's strongest management brand — better for general C-suite positioning and brand-heavy career transitions. IIM-K NAMP (₹4L, 10+ yrs exp) is specifically designed for 10+ year professionals and gives more targeted senior leadership frameworks at half the cost. Both give their respective IIM alumni status.

What is the eligibility difference between AGMP and NAMP?

IIM Ahmedabad AGMP: Graduate + 5 years work experience. IIM Kozhikode NAMP: Graduate + 10 years work experience. NAMP has a significantly higher experience requirement — this is by design. The NAMP curriculum is built specifically for the 10+ year professional; AGMP targets 5-10 years.

Which gives better career impact — IIMA AGMP or IIM-K NAMP?

For professionals with 5-8 years experience: IIMA AGMP gives stronger brand recognition and broader general management positioning. For professionals with 10+ years: NAMP's peer cohort quality and specifically calibrated curriculum often delivers more relevant career impact at half the fee. The right choice depends heavily on your experience level and specific career goal.
