IIM Kozhikode Supply Chain Analytics — What It Is

The Advanced Supply Chain Management & Role of Analytics programme by IIM Kozhikode is a 6-month executive certificate covering supply chain strategy, demand analytics, logistics optimisation, and digital supply chain technologies. ~₹1.75L + GST. Online with IIM-K campus. IIM Kozhikode is NIRF #3 Management, EQUIS + AMBA accredited. Gives IIM Kozhikode alumni status.

Curriculum — What Supply Chain Analytics Covers

  • Demand forecasting: statistical methods + ML-based forecasting for supply chain
  • Inventory optimisation: EOQ models, dynamic safety stock, AI-driven replenishment
  • Logistics and distribution: network design, last-mile optimisation, carrier selection analytics
  • Procurement analytics: supplier performance scoring, spend analysis, contract analytics
  • Digital supply chain: IoT in warehousing, track-and-trace, supply chain visibility platforms
  • Sustainability: Scope 3 emissions tracking, circular supply chain models

Who Is This For?

Best suited for: supply chain managers (3-8 years experience) wanting analytics capability + IIM credentials; procurement and sourcing professionals targeting senior roles; operations managers in manufacturing, FMCG, pharma, or e-commerce who need to lead analytics-driven supply chain decisions. Also valuable for consultants advising on supply chain transformation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Kozhikode supply chain programme worth it?

Yes — for supply chain and operations professionals, this is an excellent value credential. ₹1.75L for 6 months from NIRF #3 IIM with EQUIS+AMBA accreditation. The supply chain + analytics combination is directly relevant to supply chain leadership roles in manufacturing, FMCG, pharma, e-commerce, and logistics.

What analytics skills does this IIM-K programme teach?

Demand forecasting with statistical and ML methods, inventory optimisation analytics, logistics network optimisation, spend analytics for procurement, supply chain risk sensing, and data visualisation for supply chain KPIs. No coding required — business application of analytics tools.

IIM Kozhikode supply chain vs APICS CPIM — which is better?

Different credentials for different purposes. APICS CPIM is an internationally recognised operations credential covering supply chain management processes in depth — better for technical operations expertise globally. IIM-K Supply Chain Analytics gives IIM institutional brand + analytics focus — better for Indian corporate career advancement and supply chain leadership roles in India.
