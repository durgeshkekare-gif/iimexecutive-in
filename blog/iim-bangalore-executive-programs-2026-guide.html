IIM Bangalore — India's #2 Management School

IIM Bangalore (IIMB) is NIRF #2 in Management, Triple Crown accredited (AACSB + AMBA + EQUIS), and consistently ranked among Asia's top business schools. For working professionals, IIMB offers the PGPEM (part-time executive MBA), PGPBA (business analytics), and various executive education certificate programmes through its Centre for Executive Education.

PGPEM — IIM Bangalore's Weekend MBA

The Post Graduate Programme in Enterprise Management (PGPEM) is IIMB's part-time MBA for working professionals. It runs on weekends at the IIM Bangalore campus in Bengaluru — making it suitable only for professionals based in or willing to travel to Bengaluru. Fee: approximately ₹23-27L. Duration: 3 years. Min experience: 5 years. Gives an MBA degree equivalent to IIMB's full-time PGP.

IIM Bangalore Executive Education Certificates

IIMB's Centre for Executive Education offers shorter programmes (3-6 months) in Strategy, Finance, Marketing, Digital Business, and Leadership. These are open enrolment programmes costing ₹1.5-4L with IIMB alumni status. Availability changes annually — check the IIMB Executive Education website for current programmes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does IIM Bangalore have part-time MBA for working professionals?

Yes — IIM Bangalore PGPEM (Post Graduate Programme in Enterprise Management) is a 3-year part-time MBA running on weekends at the Bengaluru campus. Fee: ~₹23-27L. Min experience: 5 years. Requires being in or near Bengaluru for weekend classes.

IIM Bangalore or IIM Nagpur for executive MBA?

IIM Bangalore PGPEM: stronger brand (#2 vs #25 NIRF), higher fee (~₹25L vs ₹12.10L), Bengaluru-only. IIM Nagpur Blended MBA: more affordable, pan-India accessible (online + 4 visits), requires only 3 years experience. If you are in Bengaluru and can invest ₹25L: IIMB PGPEM. If you want pan-India flexibility and lower cost: IIM Nagpur.

What is PGPBA at IIM Bangalore?

PGPBA (Post Graduate Programme in Business Analytics) is IIMB's 1-year analytics programme for professionals with quantitative backgrounds. Fee: ~₹23-25L. Requires 2 years experience + strong quantitative aptitude. Gives IIMB alumni status and is highly regarded for analytics leadership roles.
