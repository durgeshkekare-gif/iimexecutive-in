Frequently Asked Questions

What is the lowest fee IIM executive program in India 2026?

The lowest fee IIM executive programmes 2026 are: IIM Mumbai Advanced Project Management & Analytics (~₹1.65L, 6 months), IIM Mumbai Corporate Strategic Finance (~₹1.75L, 6 months), and IIM Nagpur Data Science for Business (~₹1.75L, 6 months). These give IIM alumni status at the most accessible fee points.

What is the highest fee IIM executive program in India 2026?

The highest fee is IIM Nagpur Blended MBA at ₹12,10,000 (2 years) — which is also the only full MBA degree from an IIM for working professionals. Among executive certificates, IIM Ahmedabad AGMP at ₹8,00,000 (1 year) is the highest.

Are IIM executive program fees the same across batches?

No — IIM executive programme fees change between batches and are revised periodically. The fees listed here are verified from official Jaro Education programme pages as of July 2026. Always verify the current fee on the official programme page before applying.
